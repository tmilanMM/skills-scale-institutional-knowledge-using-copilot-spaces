# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies, acceptance criteria validation, and risk-based test planning. They help teams identify quality gaps early and support confident releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define a test strategy aligned with project scope, quality targets, and delivery risk
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and acceptance criteria traceability
- Perform manual QA and coordinate automated QA execution
- Identify quality gaps, defects, and release risks
- Support release readiness, smoke testing, and deployment confidence
- Participate in retrospectives to improve team quality practices

### Goals
- Ensure acceptance criteria are met before release
- Minimize defects and reduce rework
- Enable fast, confident deployments through reliable QA coverage

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Quality status updates and defect triage discussions
- Test case reviews and acceptance criteria walkthroughs
- Release checkpoints and go/no-go readiness conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to clarify testability, reproduce issues, and validate fixes before release
- Partner with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria, user expectations, and release quality thresholds
- Coordinate with Project Managers on test timelines, quality risks, and release readiness status

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, prioritization guidance, and approval authority. They help keep projects aligned with strategic objectives and ensure teams are solving the right problems.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate the business need and expected outcomes
- Approve project charters, budgets, and major scope changes
- Participate in key planning and decision gates
- Review milestone progress and provide business feedback
- Escalate blockers that require leadership support
- Champion project outcomes and organizational adoption

### Goals
- Deliver on strategic objectives and expected business value
- Minimize scope creep while maintaining predictability
- Drive adoption and support for delivered solutions

### Typical Communication
- Initiation and kickoff meetings
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Decision gates and approval checkpoints
- Incident escalation and executive alignment conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide Developers with business context that helps shape trade-off decisions and clarify the value of requested work
- Guide Product Managers on priorities, constraints, and success criteria for roadmap and scope decisions
- Support Project Managers with approvals, escalation paths, and timely decisions that keep delivery moving

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide architectural decisions, ensure technical excellence, and mitigate technical risks. They help teams balance scalability, maintainability, delivery speed, and long-term platform health.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and the overall technical approach
- Conduct technical feasibility assessments and identify implementation risks
- Establish engineering standards, practices, and reusable patterns
- Mentor Developers and support code and design reviews
- Evaluate technology choices, integrations, and trade-offs
- Support capacity planning and technical debt management

### Goals
- Deliver scalable and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned rework
- Establish reusable patterns that improve future delivery
- Build technical capability across the team

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Sprint planning and estimation conversations
- Code reviews and engineering guidance
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Developers on implementation guidance, standards, and complex technical decisions
- Help Product Managers assess feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs between scope and technical constraints
- Partner with Project Managers on delivery risk, dependency planning, and capacity assumptions that affect schedules

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical delivery by gathering requirements and analyzing workflows. They help translate stakeholder needs into clear specifications that reduce ambiguity for delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirement gathering sessions
- Document current processes, pain points, and business rules
- Define user workflows and support specification development
- Create specification documents and process artifacts
- Validate proposed solutions against documented requirements
- Identify integration impacts and downstream process changes

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address actual business problems
- Reduce ambiguity and rework through clear requirements
- Accelerate stakeholder alignment and decision-making
- Capture organizational knowledge for future use

### Typical Communication
- Requirement gathering workshops and interviews
- Specification reviews with business and technical teams
- Process documentation walkthroughs
- Acceptance testing coordination and solution validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide Developers with detailed workflows, clarified requirements, and supporting context for implementation decisions
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine requirements, acceptance criteria, and problem definitions
- Support Project Managers with dependency visibility, scope clarification, and documentation needed for planning and tracking
